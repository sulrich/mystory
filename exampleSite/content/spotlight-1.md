This is a **Spotlight** element, and it's generally used &ndash; as its name
implies &ndash; to spotlight a particular feature, subject, or pretty much
whatever. You can customize its appearance with a number of modifiers, as
well as assign it an optional `onload` or `onscroll` transition modifier
([details](#reference-spotlight)).
