This is also a **Spotlight** element, and it's here because this demo would
look a bit empty with just one spotlight. Like all spotlights, you can
customize its appearance with a number of modifiers, as well as assign it an
optional `onload` or `onscroll` transition modifier
([details](#reference-spotlight)).
